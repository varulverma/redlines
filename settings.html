In-page panels

Glass and Liquid glass let more of the page show through (Liquid is the most transparent and least legible on pure white or pure black pages). Solid panels hide the page behind them completely and are lighter on slower machines.

Motion

Reduced turns off animations, the trailing Inspect ring and smooth scrolling.

Categories

Show or hide the category picker in the Edit dialog. The new-note callout always shows its category chip: new notes use your first category below unless you open the chip's arrow and pick another. Applies to the next form you open.

Your categories

    Rename a category by editing its name; notes already in it move with it. Deleting one keeps its notes, still labelled with the old name.

      Your data
      
      
      What stays in your browser, and what leaves it. Learn more →
      
    
      
    
        
      
      • Stays localNotes, screenshots, categories and settings are saved in this browser's
        extension storage only. Marking, saving, the sidebar, the table and every file export work offline.
        • 
      
      • Enhance (only when you click it)Sends the note text, its category, the element's
        selector and the page address without its query string or #fragment to the provider
        above. Screenshots are not sent. You're asked once per provider before the first send.
        • 
      
      • Send to Google Drive (only when you click it)Uploads the Excel export, including
        screenshots and page addresses, to your Drive. You're asked once first. Opening this page
        doesn't contact Google; Disconnect revokes access and forgets the account.
        • 
      
      • AI agents (only while you turn it on)An AI tool on this computer that you started with
        your pairing code can read notes from your shared sites: text, element details, page addresses
        and, if allowed, screenshots. Redlines only talks to 127.0.0.1, but the AI tool may send
        what it reads to its own provider, as with anything else you show it. Every access is listed above.
        • 
      
      • While a Redlines panel is open on a pageThat page's own scripts can technically
        see what the panel shows (it is part of the page). Avoid opening it on sites you don't trust.

      • Removing dataDelete notes from the sidebar or table. Removing the extension
        deletes everything it stored.
