Sidebar notes + actions

Rounded cards. The left edge shows state (accent active, amber element lost, green resolved). Action hues carry meaning, never decoration: green resolve, blue copy, violet image, amber edit, pink enhance, orange agent, red delete. Every button has a one-sentence tooltip (data-tip); screenshots open large.
